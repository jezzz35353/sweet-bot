Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Sweet Discord bot ("Sweet", "the Bot", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it is handled. By using the Bot you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We only process data that Discord makes available to the Bot and that its features need:

  • Identifiers: Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs and role IDs.
  • Server member information: member lists, usernames, nicknames, avatars, roles and join dates in servers where the Bot is installed (Server Members Intent).
  • Presence information: online status and activity in servers where the Bot is installed (Presence Intent). This is processed in real time and is not stored long-term.
  • OAuth2 data: if you authorize the Bot through Discord OAuth2, the access token and the scopes you approved, used only to perform the actions you authorized.
  • Settings: configuration chosen by server administrators.

We do not read or store the content of your messages.

2. How we use information

  • to provide and operate the Bot's features;
  • to keep server configurations working;
  • to prevent abuse and keep the Bot secure;
  • to fix bugs and improve reliability.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers needed to host the Bot, or when required by law.

4. Storage and retention

Data is stored on secured servers and kept only as long as needed to provide the Bot's features. When the Bot is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted within 30 days. OAuth2 tokens are deleted when you revoke the Bot's authorization.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Remove the Bot from your server at any time.
  • Revoke OAuth2 access in Discord under User Settings → Authorized Apps.
  • Request access to, or deletion of, your data by contacting us (see below).

6. Children

The Bot is not intended for users below the minimum age required by Discord's Terms of Service.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the Bot's developer through the support server linked on the Bot's Discord profile.
